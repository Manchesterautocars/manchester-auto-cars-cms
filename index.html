Vehicle Management CMS

Private inventory system

This dashboard manages the vehicles shown on the public Manchester Auto Cars website. Publishing commits the vehicle inventory straight to GitHub, and the public site updates from it — so check your changes before you publish.

Enter CMS
Access to this CMS is controlled by Vercel's deployment protection on this project, not by a page in this app — there is intentionally no separate username/password screen here, since a client-side login check can't actually keep anyone out. See Settings for what's configured and what still needs setting up.
